- **Admiral Proudmoore's Orders** (4883) – Provided by Gar'Thok for The Admiral's Orders (2), delivered to Vol'jin in Orgrimmar

## Vyrin Swiftwind (1)
> In the novella Quest for Pandaria she tells the dwarf Baenan of her longing for her estranged husband Talithar.

- **Ol' Sooty's Head** (2713) – Dropped by Ol' Sooty; provided for Vyrin's Revenge (2), which ends with Vyrin

## Warchief Rend Blackhand (5)

- **Head of Rend Blackhand** (12630) – Drops from Rend Blackhand in Upper Blackrock Spire  
  _His head: the Blackhand brother who declared himself Warchief, then fell under Nefarian's command._
- **Blackhand Doomsaw** (12583) · gear – Drops from Rend Blackhand in Upper Blackrock Spire
- **Dal'Rend's Sacred Charge** (12940) · gear – Drops from Rend Blackhand in Upper Blackrock Spire
- **Dal'Rend's Tribal Guardian** (12939) · gear – Drops from Rend Blackhand in Upper Blackrock Spire
- **Blackhand's Command** (18987) – Letter from Rend Blackhand, carried by the Scarshield Quartermaster at Blackrock Spire  
  _His command: the Blackhand brother who refused Ner'zhul's Horde and later fell under Nefarian's command._

## Watcher Ladimore (1)
> In Terror by Torchlight she commands the Night Watch and is found mortally wounded after facing the Necromancer.

- **Archeus** (2000) · gear – Reward of A Daughter's Love, which Watcher Ladimore starts

## Wharfmaster Dizzywig (2)

- **Secure Crate** (6462) – Turn-in: brought to Dizzywig in Wharfmaster Dizzywig (Barrens)

## Zalazane (1)

- **Zalazane's Head** (4866) – Drops from Zalazane on the Echo Isles in Durotar
