# Group C critic verdict (item round, 2026-11-28)

Checked against `C_items.lua` and `C_report.md`. Every kept item ID was matched to its wiki item page (`|itemid=`); all 94 worker items had the Classic ID on the page (SoD-only items rejected). Blurbs are all 120 characters or fewer and rest on `veterans.json` text. Connections were checked on the quest/NPC pages for rejects, borderline quest rewards and all added items. Plain turn-ins and quest items handed out were accepted on the worker's quoted page line.

Rules applied to quest rewards (strict, per the extra task): a reward is kept only when the quest is about the veteran, not a generic choice reward from a chain they happen to start or end. Tyrande (Reception), Merithra (Regalia staves) and Bolvar (A Friend of the Family) were rejected as the user named them. Season of Discovery-only items were rejected (Classic season=discovery on the item page).
