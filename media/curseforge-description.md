Ever run into a random quest giver and later found out they were in a Warcraft II mission? This addon tells you, then lets you collect them all.

Warcraft Lore flags NPCs who also appear in the real-time strategy games and the novels, shows their story in a lore window, and turns finding them into a collection with achievements.

- Warcraft: Orcs & Humans
- Warcraft II: Tides of Darkness and Beyond the Dark Portal
- Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos and The Frozen Throne
- Warcraft III Reforged: Forsaken Kingdom
- Warcraft novels and short stories (labelled "Book", never presented as a game appearance)

**130 confirmed veterans, 12 memorials and 41 achievements.** From Thrall and Sylvanas to Tirion Fordring, Falstad Wildhammer and the goblins of Gadgetzan. Every one is checked against a source, and only characters you can actually meet in this version of the game are included.

## New in v0.8 and v0.7

- **Warcraft II look:** every window now looks like a remaster of the Warcraft II menus: stone frame, cracked leather, red buttons and gold text. Prefer the old violet look? One click on the **Classic** button next to the close X switches.
- **Lore Journal with 41 achievements:** milestones from 1 to 100 veterans, one per Warcraft game, one per zone, "Circles" of related characters and more. A small gold banner pops up when you earn one.
- **Memorials:** the heroes you can't meet in person still count. Uther's Tomb, the plaques in Stormwind's Valley of Heroes, Trollbane's Tomb, Grom Hellscream's monument and more are on the world map, ready to be visited.
- **Deutsch und Français:** the interface speaks German and French. The stories stay in English.

## Discover

- **Tooltip line:** veterans get one extra line, e.g. "Warcraft Veteran: WC3 · TFT", each game in its own color. Other NPCs stay clean.
- **Calm alerts:** a small glowing pill when a veteran is nearby. In cities you get one alert and one "Also nearby: …" line, not a flood.
- **World map pins:** see where veterans and memorials are, green once you've met or visited them. Hover for details, click for the story.

## Read

- **Lore window with a live 3D portrait:** the veteran as they look in WoW today, inside a ring that glows in the game's colors.
- **Story first, then "Through the ages":** every game appearance in release order with year and role, and the books as compact rows.
- **Related:** follow the thread from Thrall to Cairne to Baine, or from Tyrande to Shandris.

## Collect

- **Met counter for your whole account:** target or mouse over a veteran to tick them off. Progress is shared by all your characters, and the tooltip shows how often you met them and with which character first.
- **Veterans list** (`/wcl list` or the minimap button): search by name or zone, filter by game, zone, campaign or met status. The zone picker shows how many you've met in each zone, and "This zone" jumps to where you are.
- **Lore Journal** (`/wcl journal`, or Shift-click the minimap button): progress bars and the date you earned each achievement. 31 can be earned today; the rest open up when the level cap rises.
- Already played an older version? You get the achievements you've earned on first login.

## Make it yours

- **Two looks:** Warcraft II (default) or classic, with `/wcl theme` or the button in every window.
- **Too chatty?** `/wcl auto` turns off the alert when you target a veteran, and `/wcl alert` turns off the nearby alert. Both are also in the settings.
- **Settings page** in the game's AddOns options (`/wcl options`), and export/import of your met list as a short text.
- **Safe after patches:** if a game patch changes something, only that feature switches off, with one chat line, instead of throwing errors.

## Commands

- `/wcl` – open the story of your target
- `/wcl list` – browse all veterans
- `/wcl journal` – open the Lore Journal
- `/wcl why` – explain why your target is or isn't recognised
- `/wcl theme` – switch between the Warcraft II look and the classic look
- `/wcl options` – open the settings
- `/wcl auto` / `/wcl alert` – toggle the target alert / the nearby alert
- `/wcl export` / `/wcl import` – copy or merge your met list
- `/wcl minimap` – show or hide the minimap button
- `/wcl test`, `/wcl reset`, `/wcl id`, `/wcl debug`
